## Substantive draft

*(Preserved from the earlier `/terms` draft. Tone matches the site's existing Privacy Policy and
Affiliate Disclosure pages. Content below is a starting point for legal review, not finished
copy.)*

### 1. What JetStash is

JetStash is a UK travel-intelligence website. It researches and publishes route status, fare
patterns, booking timing and travel-readiness guidance for international journeys from UK airports.
Using the site would mean accepting these terms, once published.

### 2. What JetStash is not

JetStash is not a travel agent, tour operator or booking platform, and does not take payment or
process bookings on the site itself. It is not ATOL or ABTA protected because it does not sell
flights, package holidays or any other travel arrangement directly — that protection sits with the
airline, agent or platform a visitor actually books with. When a visitor clicks through to a partner
such as TravelUp and completes a booking, their contract is with that partner, not with JetStash.
The partner's terms, payment handling, customer service, and financial protection (if any) apply,
not JetStash's. Cross-reference the existing `/affiliate-disclosure` page for how that relationship
works and what it does and doesn't influence. *(See "Final ATOL and ABTA wording" above.)*

### 3. Accuracy of information

Route, fare, schedule and document information on the site is researched and dated, not live. Fares
are indicative and checked at a specific point in time shown on each listing; airline schedules,
visa rules and document requirements can change after they've been checked. Visitors would be asked
to always confirm the current price, schedule and entry requirements directly with the airline,
operator or relevant government before booking or travelling. Genuine errors are corrected when
found, but the site does not guarantee to be complete, current or error-free at every moment.

### 4. Travel Ready Check and document guidance

Travel Ready Check and any passport, visa or document guidance on the site are sourced from official
government pages and dated when last checked, but they are general guidance, not personal
immigration or legal advice, and they don't cover every nationality, document type or personal
circumstance. Visitors would be asked to always confirm their specific requirements directly with
the relevant embassy, consulate or official government source before travelling.

### 5. Acceptable use

A visitor would be asked not to use the site to do anything unlawful, to scrape or systematically
extract its content for republication, to attempt to disrupt or gain unauthorised access to it, or
to impersonate JetStash or misrepresent their relationship with it.

### 6. Intellectual property

The route intelligence, written content, design and branding on the site would be described as
JetStash's own work. Linking to JetStash would be welcomed; copying, republishing or systematically
reproducing its content elsewhere without asking first would not be permitted.

### 7. Liability

*(See "Final liability wording" above — not approved language.)* Because JetStash doesn't take
bookings or handle payments, it would not be liable for the acts, omissions, pricing, service
quality or financial protection of any airline, agent or platform a visitor books with after leaving
the site. The site would be provided "as is," and to the fullest extent the law allows, JetStash
would not be liable for losses arising from using it or relying on information on it. Nothing in
these terms would exclude or limit liability where the law doesn't allow it to be excluded or
limited, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud — this
carve-out is a legal requirement under UK law, not optional wording.

### 8. Changes to these terms

JetStash may update these terms as the site or its services change. A "last updated" date would show
when the page was last revised; continuing to use the site after an update would mean accepting the
current version.
