## Why the fresh fare is not a Standout, and why that is expected
Fare Watcher needs at least 3 earlier independent observations within 7 days of the candidate's booking horizon (`FARE_WATCHER_MIN_BASELINE`, `FARE_WATCHER_MAX_HORIZON_DRIFT_DAYS`). The 7 Oct fare has a horizon of 41 days; the earlier observations sit at 95, 91, 77, 62, 56 and 49 days, so none qualifies and there is no candidate. A Standout must earn its qualification naturally; it is not rescued by changing thresholds, baseline rules, booking-horizon rules, exact-match behaviour or the Standout lifecycle, and none was changed.
